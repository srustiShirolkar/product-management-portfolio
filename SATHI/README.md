# SATHI
### Continuous Eldercare Platform

**CARE. RESPONSE. CONNECTION.**

*Growing older should never mean becoming disconnected.*

An independent product exploration into building a more connected and dependable eldercare experience.

---

## The Opportunity

How can families living away from their ageing parents ensure that their healthcare, everyday assistance and emotional wellbeing are consistently taken care of?

## The Product

SATHI is a subscription-based homecare and companionship initiative, initially envisioned for Hubballi.

It brings three interconnected pillars together:

- **CARE** — Everyday health and wellbeing.
- **RESPONSE** — Emergency assistance and coordinated action.
- **CONNECTION** — Companionship, community and belonging.

## Product Experience

A connected digital experience explored through four interfaces:

**Senior Citizens · Doctors · Care Team · Family Members**
