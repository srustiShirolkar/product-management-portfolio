# Olavu Readiness Engine

### Ambulance Readiness Management Platform

An independent product exploration focused on making ambulance readiness a structured, visible and accountable process.

---

## The Operational Problem

An ambulance may be fully equipped at the time of delivery, but maintaining its readiness requires continuous checks, timely issue resolution and clear ownership.

How can hospitals ensure that their ambulances remain ready for service every day?

## The Product Approach

Olavu Readiness Engine explores a configurable platform that helps hospitals:

- Conduct ambulance readiness checks.
- Identify and document equipment or operational issues.
- Assign responsibility for corrective action.
- Access relevant SOPs and track resolution.
- Verify completion and maintain updated readiness status.

The goal is to move beyond task completion and ensure that readiness is actually verified.

## System & User Workflows

A connected experience explored through four interfaces:

**Hospital Management · Assigned Personnel · Maintenance Personnel · Hospital Administration**

The workflow connects readiness checks, issue reporting, responsibility assignment, corrective action, verification and management visibility.
